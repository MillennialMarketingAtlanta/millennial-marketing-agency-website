Founder + CEO

Christa Huffstickler

Since 2002, Christa, Founder of Millennial Marketing, has established herself as a trusted leader in creative marketing and brand development, particularly within the real estate industry, where she blends a deep understanding of sales with a strategic, data-driven marketing mindset. Just prior to joining forces with the globally renowned luxury real estate brokerage, Engel & Völkers, and building a robust new development pipeline, Christa founded Millennial Marketing to meet the evolving needs of her builder and developer clients.

Read More

Offering a seamless, one-stop solution, Millennial Marketing provides comprehensive brand development, full-scale marketing strategies, and awareness execution, all fully integrated with her sales team to ensure cohesive and impactful results. Today, the agency has evolved into a multifaceted brand, specializing across diverse industries that span far beyond real estate.

Christa's expertise in particular spans new project launches, brand development, and campaign revitalization, with a focus on positioning brands for success through data-driven strategy and real-time market insights. Through Millennial Marketing, she has orchestrated impactful campaigns for Atlanta's most prestigious real estate developments, managing over half a billion dollars in new home listings and delivering a proven track record of success.

Managing Partner

Ginger Snyder

As Managing Partner of Millennial Marketing, Ginger stands out for her ability to blend creativity with strategic thinking, bringing a unique combination of artistic vision and analytical insight to every project. Since joining Christa at the launch of the Atlanta branches of globally recognized real estate brokerage, Engel & Völkers, Ginger has played a pivotal role in shaping Millennial Marketing's creative direction and expanding its footprint across multiple industries, all while maintaining a strong presence in real estate marketing with her heavy integration within the brokerage.

Read More

With nearly a decade of experience specializing in real estate marketing, Ginger shares Christa's passion for the industry and its dynamic blend of creative strategy and sales-driven results. Her expertise in brand development and creative direction allows her to lead the team in crafting campaigns that capture the essence of each client's vision, from the initial concept to final execution, all while managing the delicate yet impactful integration between the sales team and the marketing team. Ginger's keen eye for design and deep understanding of audience behavior ensure that each campaign not only resonates with the right people at the right time but also delivers measurable, data-driven results that impact the bottom line.

Ginger's role extends beyond creative leadership, as she is deeply committed to building strong client relationships and serves as a main point of contact for Millennial Marketing's client base. By maintaining a hands-on approach, she ensures all efforts, whether focused on brand development or strategic marketing, are well executed and handled with excellence. Her ability to combine creativity, strategic thinking, and exceptional client service makes Ginger an invaluable force behind Millennial Marketing's growth and success.
