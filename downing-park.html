Downing Park

Creating a residential brand rooted in history while positioning it for the next generation of luxury homeowners.

About

Located within the sought-after Druid Hills neighborhood near Druid Hills Golf and Country Club, 1585 S. Ponce de Leon Ave is the site of the newly delivered Downing Park community, comprising 22 new-construction homes with Tudor Revival architecture and a preserved 1914 mansion converted to luxury condominiums.

Our Role

Millennial Marketing partnered with Reed Associates to name, position, brand, and launch Downing Park while sustaining sales support through the full life of the development.

Project Impact

A Cohesive Launch Platform with lasting value

    The brand framework was built to support future marketing phases without losing clarity or quality.
