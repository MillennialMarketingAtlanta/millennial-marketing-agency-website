The Waldorf Astoria

Bringing the final residences at Waldorf Astoria Atlanta to market through strategic positioning, elevated creative, and globally aligned campaigns.

About

As the final Private Residences at Waldorf Astoria Atlanta remained available, the development required a refreshed marketing approach that honored one of the world's most recognizable luxury hospitality brands while creating renewed excitement among discerning buyers.

Our Role

Working alongside ownership and the Waldorf Astoria brand, we developed a comprehensive strategy to reposition the remaining inventory, elevating each residence through thoughtful storytelling, bespoke collateral, and multi-channel campaigns designed to reach affluent audiences locally and nationally. Every touchpoint, from print advertising and editorial photography to digital campaigns and individualized sales collateral, was designed to reflect the sophistication of the Waldorf Astoria brand while creating a distinct identity for each available home.

Elevating Every Residence

Tailored Marketing Assets

Each remaining residence received its own suite of marketing assets tailored to its floor plan, views, and unique characteristics.

This included professionally designed property brochures, custom floor plan presentations, editorial-inspired photography, virtual staging, cinematic video production, and digital marketing assets that presented every home as an individual luxury offering rather than simply another available unit.

Lifestyle-Driven Marketing Materials

Local + National Exposure

Reaching Qualified
Luxury Buyers

To maximize visibility among affluent audiences, we developed coordinated print and digital advertising campaigns across premier luxury publications, including The Wall Street Journal and Mansions Global. These placements extended the reach of the campaign beyond Atlanta while maintaining a consistent visual identity across every channel.

Project Impact

The Formal Closeout of an Icon

Through a coordinated repositioning strategy, premium creative execution, and targeted luxury advertising, the remaining inventory of the Waldorf Astoria Private Residences was successfully sold, bringing one of Atlanta's most iconic residential offerings to a close.

  • Complete Inventory Sold

    A fully integrated marketing strategy helped drive the successful sellout of the remaining residences, concluding the final chapter of the Waldorf Astoria Atlanta.

  • Elevated Luxury Positioning

    Every marketing touchpoint—from print collateral to digital campaigns—reinforced the property's world-class reputation while attracting qualified luxury buyers.

  • Local + National Reach

    Strategic placements across premier luxury publications expanded visibility beyond Atlanta, introducing the residences to affluent audiences across the country.

  • A Lasting Marketing Legacy

    The campaign created a cohesive brand presence that honored the Waldorf Astoria legacy while establishing a benchmark for luxury residential marketing.

The result was more than a successful sellout—it was the thoughtful conclusion of an iconic Atlanta address through strategic storytelling, elevated design, and targeted luxury marketing.
